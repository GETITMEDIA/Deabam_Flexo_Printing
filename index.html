WELCOME TO DEABAM FLEXO PRINTERS

Deabam Flexo Printers, established in 1988 and based in Pondicherry, India, is a leading manufacturer of high-quality printed plastic bags and pouches. Specializing in flexographic printing, the company produces a wide range of packaging solutions—including PP, LDPE, and HDPE bags, carry bags, and customized pouches—for various industries. Committed to quality, precision, and timely delivery, Deabam Flexo Printers combines modern printing technology with durable materials to meet the diverse packaging needs of its clients.

Deabam Flexo Printers specializes in high-quality flexo printing, producing sharp, vibrant, and durable prints on a variety of materials. They work with a wide range of substrates, including LDPE, HDPE, and PP, providing versatile packaging solutions. The company offers customized packaging with tailored designs and sizes to meet specific client requirements, while their efficient production process ensures timely delivery for bulk orders. Additionally, they follow eco-friendly practices by using modern, environmentally responsible inks and printing technology.
