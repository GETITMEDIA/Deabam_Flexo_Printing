Welcome to Deabam

A Pondicherry packaging plant built on print quality

Established in 1988, Deabam Flexo Printers manufactures a complete range of printed plastic bags and pouches for food, retail, FMCG, textile and industrial customers. Everything from film selection to plate mounting and final gusseting happens under one roof, so colour and dimension stay consistent from the first bag to the last.

We print on LDPE, HDPE, HM and PP substrates with modern, environmentally responsible inks, and we build every job to the size, gauge and artwork our customer specifies.

  • Custom sizes & gauges
  • Multi-colour registration
  • Food-grade film options
  • Bulk production capacity

Why Deabam

Hi-tech printers, dependable output

Our services

Choose the film that fits your product

Four core substrates, each printed and converted in-house.

Poly-Propylene (PP)

PP printed bags of supreme quality, manufactured to the clarity and stiffness that graphic-arts grade polypropylene is developed for — printing, packaging and promotional applications alike.

  • High-clarity finish that shows off artwork
  • Strong moisture barrier for dry goods
  • Used for sugar, spices, grains and textiles
Enquire about PP bags

High Molecular (HM)

Premium printed HM bags built for strength at a thin gauge — the economical choice when you need a light bag that still carries weight.

  • Excellent strength-to-thickness ratio
  • Ideal for cleaning powders and soap covers
  • Available in clear and opaque film
Enquire about HM bags

Lower Density (LD)

Our LDPE range prints beautifully and flexes without splitting. Flexographic and allied printing machines let us create attractive, elegant designs on soft, durable film.

  • Soft, flexible feel with high tear resistance
  • D-cut and loop-handle shopping bag formats
  • Suits liners, covers and retail packing
Enquire about LD bags

Carry Bags

Best-in-class printed carry bags for retail counters. Shopping bags remain in great demand because they look good and are easy to carry — ours are built with reinforced handles for real-world loads.

  • D-cut, U-cut and loop handle options
  • Brand artwork printed edge to edge
  • Sized for garments, groceries and gifting
Enquire about carry bags

How we work

From artwork to dispatch in four steps

A clear process, so you always know what happens next.

Share your requirement

Send us the bag type, size, gauge, quantity and artwork. Not sure yet? Tell us what you're packing and we'll advise.

Quote & sample approval

You receive a costed quotation. On approval we prepare plates and a print sample for your written sign-off.

Print & convert

The job runs on our flexo line, then moves to cutting, sealing and handle punching with checks at each stage.

Pack & deliver

Bags are counted, bundled and dispatched to your godown on the committed date.
