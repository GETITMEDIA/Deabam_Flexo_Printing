Medical waste packaging

Built for waste that cannot leak

Medical waste garbage bags are ideal for hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics, emergency rooms, nursing homes and any medical facility. They are suited to the disposal of waste that would be considered bio-hazardous or infectious, and are manufactured with a durable reinforced gusseted bottom seal.

We produce them in the colours your facility's segregation policy requires, in the gauges that suit the weight being carried, and with bio-hazard symbols or facility markings printed on request.

  • Reinforced gusseted bottom seal
  • Colour-coded segregation
  • Heavy-gauge film options
  • Bio-hazard marking on request
  • Bin-matched sizes
  • Bulk supply contracts
