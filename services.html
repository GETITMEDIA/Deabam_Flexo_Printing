What we do

Printed packaging, made to order

Six production lines of work, all running off the same in-house flexo capability.

PP

Poly-Propylene (PP)

A special formulation of polypropylene developed for the graphic arts industry — printing, packaging and promotional applications. High clarity, good stiffness and an excellent moisture barrier.

HM

Flexo Printed HM

High molecular film printed on our flexographic machines to create attractive, elegant designs. Widely used for packing a broad variety of products at a light gauge.

LD

Flexo Printed LD

Low density polythene bags with high-quality manufactured loops that give the bag its necessary strength — soft to handle, resistant to tearing.

Our process

How an order runs

Specification

We agree the film, gauge, bag size, handle style and number of print colours.

Plates & proof

Artwork is separated, plates are made and a printed sample goes to you for sign-off.

Production

The run is printed, cut, sealed and punched, with in-line quality checks throughout.

Dispatch

Counted, bundled and delivered on the date confirmed in your quotation.
