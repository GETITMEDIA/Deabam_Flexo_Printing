Our story

A personalised approach to every packaging order

Established in 1988, Deabam Flexo Printers is a well-known manufacturer of a premium range of printed plastic bags and pouches. Our bags are made from high-quality flexible plastic lay-flat tubing, and the features that make them stand out in the market are their long-lasting nature, temperature resistance and strength.

The range manufactured at our plant includes printed plastic bags, printed HDPE bags, printed LDPE bags, PP printed bags and flexo printed bags — all made using best-in-class material and print inks. Our bags are widely used for packing grocery, food, textiles, sugar, spices, bleaching and cleaning powder, hospital waste covers, X-ray covers, pulses and resin.

The professionals in our team have worked in this industry for a long time and bring innovative design thinking to every job. We keep expert designers, machine operators, quality-assurance staff and support workforce in-house, which is how we hold a consistent standard from the first order to the hundredth repeat.

Essential functions & key operations

What actually happens between your purchase order and your delivery note.

  • Set up and operate bag machines and support equipment to convert HDPE film into finished bags.
  • Read and verify every line order and docket before the run begins.
  • Interpret the printing job requirements against the approved specification.
  • Load film and adjust the bag line for the ordered size and gauge.
  • Mount cylinders and adjust pressure, tension and ink distribution.
  • Troubleshoot stoppages and breakdowns to protect the delivery date.
  • Perform quality checks on material being produced, with related documentation.
  • Complete daily production paperwork and maintain a clean, safe work area.
