Personalized Approach

Established in the year 1988, DEABAM FLEXO PRINTER is a famous manufacturer of premium range of printed plastic bags and pouches. These bags that are manufactured by us are made of premium quality of flexible plastic lay flat tubings. The features that make our products stand out in the market include long-lasting nature, temperature resistance and strength.

The wide range of bags which is manufactured at our plant includes Printed Plastic Bags, Printed HDPE Bags, Printed LDPE Bags, PP Printed Bags and Flexo Printed Bags. All these bags are made using the best-in-class material and print cartridges. The wide range of bags manufactured by our organization is widely used in packing grocery, foods, textiles, sugar, spices, bleaching powder, cleaning powder, hospital waste covers, x-ray covers, pulses, resin and for various other purposes. We have a well-trained team of expert professionals, who help us in manufacturing incomparable range of printed bags for our esteemed clients.

The professionals hired by our organisation have been working in this industry for a long time and bring innovative designs for printing a distinctive range of printed bags. We have hired expert designers, manufacturers, quality assuring agents and other relevant workforce in our team. All of these professionals are competent in rendering best-in-domain services along with finely crafted range of printed bags for our esteemed clients.
